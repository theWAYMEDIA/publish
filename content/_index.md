theWAY Media is the publishing imprint of the Wayist community — nearly eighty books and well
over 150 songs, spanning core teaching texts, biography, children's stories, historical works,
and poetry, alongside music carrying Wayist teaching into sound across country, Norteño,
corridos, Afrikaans folk, house, and devotional genres. All of it is brought to life by
teachers, authors, and artists across the Wayist community, and made available in paperback,
ebook, and audiobook formats, and through music retailers including Spotify, Apple Music, and
Amazon.

Every work published under theWAY Media is donated in full — copyright and license — to
Caminismo A.C., so it stays freely available to the Wayist community rather than owned by any
individual contributor. Browse the full [book catalog](/books/), the [music catalog](/music/),
or meet the [authors and artists](/authors/) behind it.
