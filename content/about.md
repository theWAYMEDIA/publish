theWAY Media publishes books on Wayism and related teachings — from core teaching texts to
biography, children's stories, and historical works — bringing together authors and teachers
from the Wayist community.

Our books are available in paperback, ebook, and audiobook formats through major retailers.
Most titles are available for translation.

For submissions, rights inquiries, or general questions, reach out via the online community:
[wayist.life](https://wayist.life).

## Published by theWAY Media

theWAY Media, wayism.net, is the publishing imprint of El Camino de Caminismo Universal,
Asociación Civil ("Caminismo A.C."), Mexico — [caminismo.org](https://caminismo.org). Every
work published under theWAY Media is donated in full — copyright and license — to Caminismo
A.C., so it stays freely available to the Wayist community rather than owned by any individual
contributor.

## A Cultural Mission

Caminismo A.C.'s stated legal purpose is cultural, and that shows up directly in what we
publish. A large share of our catalog — books, poems, and songs alike — comes from artists
who live and write across the US/Mexico border, and it consistently holds up Mexico's ancient
and diverse culture in a positive light: its regional music traditions, its layered indigenous
and mestizo roots, its faith, its hospitality. The values that recur through the catalog —
family that stays close, elders who are respected, work done honestly because it is yours to
do — are offered as more than local color. They are presented as a working answer to problems
much of Western society is still trying to solve, and as something worth learning from rather
than leaving behind.
