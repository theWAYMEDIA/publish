## Wayist Insight

This mantra is used by energy healers to attune themselves to their Tara before a healing session. Its three Sanskrit lines carry a teaching central to Wayist pneumatherapy: **the healer does not heal — divine energy heals through the healer.**

Line by line, the mantra trains the practitioner to step aside:

**Om jyotiḥ prāṇena vahati** — *Light is carried through the breath.* The healing light (jyotiḥ) flows through prāṇa, the life-force. It is not summoned or generated by the healer — it is received and carried.

**Rogaṃ hṛdayena śodhyate** — *Affliction is purified through the heart.* The heart serves as the channel through which purification happens. The healer opens the heart-space; the cleansing itself is not a personal act of will.

**Divya śaktiḥ pravartatā svaḥa** — *Divine power flows forth — svaḥa.* The energy that does the healing work is divya śaktiḥ — divine power from above. The closing svaḥa is an offering-word, directing the prayer upward and acknowledging its heavenly source.

The closing English lines make the teaching explicit: this is power that flows *to us from heaven*, channelled through the healer in sacred partnership with their Tara.
