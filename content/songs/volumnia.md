There was a woman in old Rome

Who saw what no one else could see

That when the fertile earth goes fallow

The soul breaks free

Your body did what nature demanded

Now that contract is through

Time for the deeper work to start in you

Volumnia Magna knew

What they tried to hide from you

When the mothering is through

"Don't kill your fire

When biology gives up"

Your sensuous life begins anew

They told you to fade polite

To wither and dry, deny the light

Deny the wisdom earned at night

And call it proper, call it done

But you came here for more than breeding

In a body made for bliss

To die inside at fifty is to miss…

This is the soul-phase, darling

The reason that you came

To swim in your own darkness

Your secret, sacred game

The fertile phase is over

Now the real work starts

She taught them in her temple:

"You weren't born just to reproduce

You were born to know yourself

Through pleasure. Through the truth."

But most choose candy, cake

And stories of another's sins

They imprisoned Volumnia Magna

For teaching women this:

That after all the bleeding ends

Comes the soul's deep kiss

That sensuality, unchained from fertility

Is nature's final gift, her purest prayer

A woman living fully in her senses

Is awesome, and easy to desire

Volumnia Magna knew

What they tried to hide from you

When the mothering is through

"Don't kill your fire

Your soul must go higher"

Your sensuous life begins anew

This is the doorway, lady

Not the closing of the book

To fade into the grey

Is to leave your soul unfurled

The menopause is not an end…

It's the soul's turn to swim

In the secret desires within
