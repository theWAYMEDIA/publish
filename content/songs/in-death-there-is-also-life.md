My sister Adele... she write this down

The day we lost our Tracey in Johannesburg

We were on the farm that day

Bleak highveld winter

She was chopping out a dead tree when word came

This is her testimony

Bleak highveld winter's day

Unknown graves

Forgotten loved ones

Chopping out a dead tree

Word came

Tracey died

Through tears I chop

Why Lord... why

Why... why

Why her

Why now

The ax-head came loose

Another pain

Another knowing

Tears the veils

Climbing down the tree

Apologizing

For each blow of the ax

Finding footing

Hand over hand

Finding acceptance

Death is life too

Holding the dead tree close

Both crying for life

Blood from the cut

Spilling life

Welcoming earth

I kiss the tree

A brief moment

We share life

That passes

I pray Lord why

My need

I know

Death is life too
