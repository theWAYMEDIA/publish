Buyer Be-Aware 

Categorised in the genre, Children's Books for Strange Adults, this book is ideal for children ages 40 to ancient- and-strange. The book is intended for individuals who can handle plants and animals without social filters, who fearlessly challenge popular narratives, not hesitating to call humans dumb as f&%k. It is a nuanced period piece set in an emotionally bloody time in history shortly before the 2024 / 5 elections, which changed the UK, USA and Canada forever.  

I welcomed the opportunity to write the biography High Mountain Story Uncle; it was something to do other than live the privilege of busting your ass to pay the taxes the bureaucrats need to cement their elite status and fund people who don’t bust their ass to pay the taxes. 

I knew the assignment would be challenging. Our whimsical Uncle is, after all, the most enigmatic figure known to the literary world, but I did not account for the fact that the village of Nihnuta itself is disturbingly otherworldly. While I expected Uncle to be eccentric, I was not psychologically prepared to spend time in a community of individuals who deliberately teeter on the very edge of sanity and reality, and whimsically plays hopscotch over that harrowing edge. The village exists as a haven of wisdom where different views of reality are cherished, and a joyful and authentic worldview is embraced. It changed me like a circumcision, permanently, everything that truly matters in life now looks different. 

I hope to besmirch you with these tales because I don’t want to be the one person outside Nihnuta to live the whimsey and joy of life all by my lonesome. 

Have fun! I absolutely had a lot of fun writing it.
