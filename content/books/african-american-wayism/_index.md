### Rediscover your spiritual birthright beyond religious boundaries

In a world that fragments identity and disconnects us from our deeper wisdom, "African American Wayism" offers Black families a path of integration, healing, and authentic power. Drawing from ancient spiritual principles that predate modern religious divisions, author Mabel Thompson illuminates how Wayist teachings align with traditional African wisdom while providing practical tools for navigating contemporary challenges.

Unlike conventional religions, Wayism is:

- Not a belief system demanding faith, but a practical path of awareness and transformation

- Free from hierarchical structures, dogma, and required rituals

- Focused on personal experience rather than performing

- Compatible with various cultural expressions and individual journeys

- Centered on developing energy awareness and personal wisdom, not following external authority

This groundbreaking guide reveals:

- How ancient African spiritual practices embodied Wayist principles of energy awareness, interconnection, and purposeful existence

- Practical approaches to conscious parenting that shield children from harmful social conditioning while nurturing their divine potential

- Ways to connect with Divine Parents and personal spiritual guides who offer unconditional love and guidance

- How the Wayist concept of Udbhu-kanti (finding beauty in authenticity) liberates families from consumer culture's pressure to define worth through acquisition

- Energy healing practices that counter the noise, fragmentation, and digital disruption of modern American life

Rediscovering ancient wisdom across cultures:

- Wayism honors the essential balance of divine feminine and masculine energies, recognizing God our Mother and God our Father as complementary spiritual forces in charge our heaven, and from the Source flows the cosmic Yin and Yang present in all creation.

- These same principles appear across world wisdom traditions—in ancient Chinese Taoism, Indian spiritual teachings, and indigenous African practices.

- Most importantly, Wayism represents the original teachings of Lord Jesus before Paul transformed these teachings into what became Christianity, removing many key elements including the divine feminine and energy-based spiritual practices.

Wayism stands apart from religions by recognizing that spiritual wisdom comes from within, not from outside authorities. It honors the soul's inherent capacity to evolve toward its highest expression without intermediaries. Rather than emphasizing sin, salvation, or divine judgment, Wayism focuses on spiritual development through conscious living and energy awareness.

Neither a rejection of contemporary reality nor an idealistic retreat to the past, this book offers a balanced path forward—one that honors ancestral wisdom while embracing the unique spiritual journey of each family member.

For African Americans seeking a spiritual approach that:

- Resonates with their heritage without religious baggage

- Supports their children's development in a challenging world

- Offers practical tools for navigating systemic challenges

- Empowers through energy awareness rather than religious conformity

- Honors both divine feminine and masculine principles equally

- Connects to the original spiritual teachings Jesus shared before they were altered

"African American Wayism" provides not just information but transformation—a return to the wholeness that has always been our birthright. It's an invitation to reclaim spiritual autonomy while honoring the wisdom traditions that sustained our ancestors through centuries of challenge and triumph.
