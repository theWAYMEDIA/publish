You're not doing it wrong. You were given the wrong map.

For 1,600 years — since Augustine of Hippo collapsed the soul/spirit distinction in 400 CE — Western culture has been handing seekers psychological tools and calling them spiritual ones. Meditation for stress relief. Mindfulness for focus. Energy work as metaphor. Prayer as positive thinking. All valuable. None of them spirit-development.

*They Stole Your Spirit* restores what was lost.

Drawing on the ancient trichotomy recognized across Hebrew, Greek, Vedic, Taoist, and Buddhist traditions — body, soul, and spirit as genuinely distinct aspects of human nature — this book gives you back the framework your culture should never have taken. You will understand why the mystics you've been reading weren't speaking metaphorically. Why your meditation practice feels incomplete. Why the spiritual realm the great teachers described is real, not poetic.

Jean Prieur du Plessis maps the Butterfly Path — humanity's original framework for spirit-mind development — with unflinching clarity. This is not self-help. It is not religion. It is the orientation for authentic spiritual work that everything else has been trying to be.

For those who suspect there is more. There is.
