In the intricate weave of spiritual wisdom, there exists an extraordinary relationship between humans and divine guides known as Taras - celestial beings whose sole purpose is to shepherd souls through the transformative journey called the Butterfly Path. "theWAY of the Divine Taras" unveils this sacred connection, offering readers an intimate glimpse into a spiritual practice that transcends conventional religious boundaries. 

From the moment of our first human incarnation, each of us has been assigned a personal Tara - a divine companion who remains steadfast through countless lifetimes. These luminous beings serve as bridges between the material and spiritual realms, adapting their guidance to meet our evolving needs as we progress from spiritual infancy to awakened awareness. 

This groundbreaking work reveals: 

- The origin of the Taras, beginning with Mari of Magadha in the 1st century 

- Ancient techniques for establishing conscious connection with your personal Tara 

- The role of Taras in spiritual protection and energy healing 

- Sacred mantras and yantras for deepening your divine relationship 

- How to recognize authentic spiritual guidance and avoid deception 

- The profound difference between soul-mind religions and spirit-mind spirituality 

Through intimate letters from and to the Taras, readers discover the tender, powerful bond possible between human and divine. These communications offer unprecedented insights into the nature of spiritual guidance and the transformation of consciousness possible through this sacred relationship. 

The book explains how Taras operate primarily through yin energy - the subtle, intuitive force that works in whispers rather than shouts, in shadows rather than spotlight. This approach makes their guidance easy to miss in our yang-dominated world, yet incredibly powerful when properly understood and accessed. 

"theWAY of the Divine Taras" is not just another book about angels or spirit guides. It presents a sophisticated understanding of multi-dimensional consciousness and humanity's potential to evolve beyond current limitations. Readers learn how Taras assist in awakening and developing our spirit-minds (Vishuddhi, Ajna, and Sahasrara chakras) while helping us navigate the challenges of physical incarnation. 

Whether you're a seasoned spiritual practitioner or newly awakening to divine guidance, this work offers practical wisdom for deepening your connection to the sacred. It explains how to create sacred space, use visualization effectively, work with energy, and develop discernment in spiritual matters. 

Most importantly, it reminds us that we are never truly alone. Each human carries within them a spark of divine potential, watched over and nurtured by beings of infinite compassion and wisdom. As our world faces unprecedented challenges, the time has come to consciously embrace this divine partnership and step into our full spiritual potential. 

This book is both a practical manual and a love letter to the divine - one that could transform your understanding of spiritual guidance and your place in the cosmic dance of consciousness evolution.
