Embark on a profound spiritual journey with "theWAY to Know Your Path" by Salvar Dàosenglu, a transformative book that explores life's most essential questions. Through the eyes of a young woman seeking purpose and meaning after a life-altering experience, readers are guided by the enigmatic Uncle Krishna on a path of self-discovery and enlightenment. 

Dàosenglu, the collective voice of Wayist monks and scholars, delves into the ancient wisdom of the Butterfly Path—a universal journey of the soul that all humans undertake. With poetic prose and deep insights, the book illuminates the nature of soul and spirit, the role of karma in our lives, and the ultimate destiny that awaits us. 

"theWAY to Know Your Path" is not merely a philosophical exploration; it is an invitation to embark on the greatest adventure of our lives: the journey of spiritual awakening. Through the teachings of Wayism, readers will learn the spiritual energy powers that come from the frequencies of humbleness, simplicity, and kindness as they navigate the challenges and joys of the human experience. Learn also about the universal spiritual Path, the Purpose of Life, and how we make the Meaning of our life — the Path that all humans traverse. 

Whether you are a seasoned spiritual seeker or just beginning to question life's deeper mysteries, this book offers a beacon of hope and guidance. Discover your true purpose, unravel the grand tapestry of existence, and awaken to the infinite wisdom that resides within.
