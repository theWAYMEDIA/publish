A practical guide for African American families seeking spiritual practices that honor their heritage while providing tools for conscious parenting, authentic living, and energetic well-being.

In "Spiritual Homecoming," elder healer Mabel Thompson bridges ancient African wisdom with the Wayist spiritual path, offering African American families practical tools for soul nourishment in a challenging world. From conscious parenting approaches that shield children from harmful conditioning to energy practices that counter digital fragmentation, this comprehensive guide addresses the unique spiritual needs of African American families. Neither rejecting modern life nor idealizing the past, Thompson provides balanced guidance for those seeking a spiritual approach that honors their heritage while supporting their journey toward authentic power, divine connection, and familial harmony.
